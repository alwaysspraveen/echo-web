# Code of Conduct

Resona is built by and for people who love music. Everyone is welcome here: be kind, be helpful, and assume good intent.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem simple.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior of any kind (race, gender, sexuality, religion, disability, age, nationality or anything else)
- Sexual content or unwanted attention
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Using Resona's spaces to share pirated content or links to it

## Where this applies

All Resona spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (it's private), or to the maintainer, [@alwaysspraveen](https://github.com/alwaysspraveen). Reports are handled confidentially.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules, depending on how serious it is. Usually a warning comes first; serious or repeated problems can lead to a permanent ban.
