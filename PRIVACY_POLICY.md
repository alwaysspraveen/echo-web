# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 November 2026**

Resona is a music app for Mac, Windows and the web. This policy explains what data Resona handles and where it goes. In short: **Resona has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and your library stays on your device.**

## What stays on your device

These are stored only on your computer (in the app's local storage), never on a Resona server:

- Liked songs, playlists and listening history
- Offline downloads (audio, artwork and lyrics)
- Settings (theme, quality, equalizer, FLAC Mode and so on)
- Resona Brain's listening stats (how long you listened to each song and what you skipped), used only to pick songs for you on this device

Uninstalling Resona, or clearing the browser's site data for the web version, deletes them.

## What Resona does not collect

- No account, name, email or phone number
- No analytics, tracking, advertising IDs or crash reports
- No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone

## Services Resona connects to

To play music, Resona talks to third-party services. They receive the request (and, as with any internet request, your IP address) and handle it under their own privacy policies.

| Service | When | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube Music** | Browsing, searching, playing | Search terms, the songs and pages you open |
| **Audio extension servers** (FLAC and 320 kbps sources, Settings → Extensions) | When a song can be upgraded, or in FLAC Mode | The song's title, artist and length |
| **LRCLIB** (lrclib.net) | Showing lyrics | The song's title, artist and length |
| **Apple Music** (public catalog) | Canvas motion artwork, if on | The song's title and artist |
| **Listen Together server** | Only while you're in a room | The room code, the name you enter, and what's playing in the room |
| **GitHub** | Checking for updates (desktop) | Only the request for the latest version |
| **Cast devices on your Wi-Fi** | Only while casting (desktop) | The song and its artwork, sent to your own TV or speaker |
| **Voice search** | Only when you press the mic | Web (Chrome): your voice is sent to Google's speech service. Desktop: recognized **on your computer**; the voice model is downloaded once from Hugging Face, and no audio leaves your device |

Resona's own server runs **on your computer** (desktop app) or wherever you run it yourself (web version). It passes requests to the services above and keeps nothing.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** only for voice search, only when you press the mic button.
- **Local network (Mac):** only to find Cast devices on your Wi-Fi.

## Children

Resona isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.
